Lab 2: Phish Hunter & URL X-Ray Scanner

Lesson 1 · Concept: Social Engineering & Deceptive Hyperlinks

Challenge Active
  • Lab Objective

    Inspect incoming emails and chat links before clicking. Spot false urgency and typosquatted domains (e.g. g00gle.com).

  • The Cursor Hover Technique

    Always place your mouse over a button without clicking to view the actual URL revealed in the bottom status bar.

  • Online Reference Tool

    Google Phishing Quiz · Google Interland: Reality River

Lab 3: The Secret Code Breakers

Lesson 1 · Concept: Data Encryption & Public Wi-Fi Snooping

Challenge Active
  • Lab Objective

    Observe how unencrypted data on open Wi-Fi can be read in plain text by hackers, and how Caesar shift encryption scrambles the message.

  • HTTPS & Ciphers

    The padlock icon on websites means data is converted to ciphertext using secret mathematical keys before traveling across the internet.

  • Online Reference Tool

Lab 4: Trojan Horse & The Fake Download Minefield

Lesson 2 · Concept: Drive-By Malware & Rogue Browser Extensions

Challenge Active
  • Lab Objective

    Websites offering free game mods often hide dangerous malware inside flashing download buttons. Identify the safe file source!

  • Rule of Third-Party Downloads

    Never install `.exe` or `.apk` files without teacher or parent permission. Official verified publishers display file signatures.

  • Online Reference Tool

    BBC Bitesize Cyber Security Challenge · PBS NOVA Malware Defense

Lab 5: The OSINT Detective & Privacy Shield

Lesson 2 · Concept: Personally Identifiable Information (PII) & Digital Footprints

Challenge Active
  • Lab Objective

    Discover how hackers gather pieces of your identity from background details in casual photos, and learn how to redact them.

  • The Golden PII Rule

    Never share your school crest, home address, birthday, or phone number in online games or social platforms.

Lab 6: The Two-Lock Safe Room & Chromebook Lockdown

Lesson 2 · Concept: Multi-Factor Authentication (2FA) & Session Security
